This tool works only on evidence you give it: an export of your resource inventory, or a Terraform state or plan. It never asks for credentials and never connects to your cloud. Everything is held in memory, is returned only to this browser session, and expires after two hours or when you delete it. The answer is not an audit.

1. What is built: your evidence

Add an export of your Azure resources: the JSON from az graph query or the Resource Graph Explorer (an array, or an object with a data array). A Terraform state or plan (terraform show -json) is accepted too, but this version does not read Terraform yet: it is recorded with a fingerprint and any claim about it stays "unknown". Remove secrets from a Terraform state first. Up to 5 files, 5 MB and 5,000 rows.

    Download the sample export

    2. What you decided: your architecture decision

    A decision says what should be true, as claims such as "data stores have no public access". The check reports, for each resource, whether what is built supports or contradicts each claim. Start from the starter or a sample, or upload your own decision file. The lab keeps it as a draft decision record; it never accepts a decision for you.

    Download the starter decision
